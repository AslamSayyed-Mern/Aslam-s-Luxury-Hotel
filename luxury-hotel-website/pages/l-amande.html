French Craftsmanship

Elegant Pastries & Royal Afternoon Tea

Step into L'Amande, a Parisian-inspired tea salon featuring marble countertops, gold leaf mirrors, and freshly baked viennoiseries. Every morning, Master Pastry Chefs craft delicate éclairs, fruit tarts, and buttery croissants using imported French AOP butter.

Indulge in our famous Royal Afternoon Tea experience, featuring silver tiered trays of finger sandwiches, warm scones, and single-estate loose leaf teas.

Confectionery Secrets

Handcrafted Macarons & Valrhona Chocolates

Discover our signature macaron gallery with seasonal flavors such as Salted Caramel Butter, Rose & Raspberry, and Sicilian Pistachio. Each confection is presented in luxury velvet gift boxes perfect for souvenirs and celebrations.

  • 16 Signature Macaron Flavors Made Daily
  • Valrhona Single-Origin Chocolate Pralines
  • Custom Celebration Cake Orders

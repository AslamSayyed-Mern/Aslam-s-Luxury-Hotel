Elevated Nights

Panoramic City Vistas & Starry Skies

Perched on the 18th floor, Celestial Rooftop Lounge offers unobstructed 360-degree views of the city skyline. Relax in luxury velvet cabanas surrounded by glowing fire pits and ambient lounge beats.

As twilight sets in, enjoy hand-crafted signature infusions and rare vintage champagnes under a starlit canopy.

Mixology Mastery

Botanical Infusions & Mediterranean Tapas

Our mixologists elevate cocktail culture with bespoke liquid nitrogen infusions, house-distilled elixirs, and rare single malts. Pair your drinks with gourmet Mediterranean small plates created for sharing.

  • Signature Nitrogen-Chilled Cocktails
  • Gourmet Iberian & Mediterranean Small Plates
  • Sunset DJ Sets & Live Saxophone Performances

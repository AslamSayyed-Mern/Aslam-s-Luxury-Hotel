Centuries of Passion

The Heart of Italian Hospitality

Via Milano celebrates the soul of Italian culinary traditions. Led by native Italian chefs, our kitchen crafts daily fresh egg pastas, wood-fired Neapolitan sourdough, and slow-braised ossobuco using imported Parmigiano Reggiano, extra virgin olive oils, and seasonal truffles.

Enclosed by warm brick archways, flickering candle sconces, and private wine alcoves, Via Milano offers a romantic ambiance for unforgettable dinners.

Vintage Enoteca

400-Label Italian Wine Vault

Immerse yourself in our temperature-controlled glass enoteca featuring rare Barolos, Super Tuscans, and vintage Prosecco reserve bottles curated directly from historic Italian vineyards.

  • Hand-Selected Tuscan & Piedmont Vintages
  • Private Wine & Artisan Cheese Tastings
  • Sommelier Pairing Recommendations

Hand-Rolled Pasta • Piedmont White Truffle

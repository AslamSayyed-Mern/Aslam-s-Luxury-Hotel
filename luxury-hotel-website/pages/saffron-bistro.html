Centuries of Royalty

Flavours Inspired by Indian Royal Courts

Saffron Fine Dining pays homage to the legendary kitchens of Indian maharajas. Master chefs draw upon ancient Awadhi dum-cooking techniques, slow-simmering copper handis, and authentic tandoori clay ovens to infuse every dish with aromatic spices.

Set within a palatial dining room featuring brass jali screens, velvet seating, and carved wooden columns, Saffron turns every meal into a royal feast.

Culinary Masterpieces

Slow-Cooked Dum Biryanis & Clay Oven Kebabs

Experience the magic of authentic Dum Pukht cooking, where rice, tender meats, and fragrant spices are sealed in clay pots with dough dough and slow-cooked over glowing embers.

  • Royal Awadhi Mutton Dum Biryani
  • Melt-in-Mouth Galouti Kebabs on Sheermal
  • House-Blended Organic Kashmiri Saffron Gravies
